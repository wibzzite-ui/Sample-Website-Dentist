Our Story

Welcome to KlayBar

Welcome to KlayBar, a one-of-a-kind Fusion Restaurant & Art Café in Bangalore, where food meets creativity!

Located in JP Nagar, Bengaluru, we offer a unique experience that blends gourmet dining with hands-on pottery and ceramic painting. Whether you're here to savor handcrafted pizzas, fresh hydroponic produce, or unwind with a pottery session, KlayBar is the perfect spot to eat, create, and connect.

Designed as a "Third Place" — a space beyond home and work — KlayBar is ideal for college students, professionals, remote workers, solo travelers, and art enthusiasts seeking a refreshing break.
