About "Fursat"

An Evening of Soulful Poetry & Ghazals

Poetry isn't just words — it's an experience, a feeling, and a journey into the depths of the soul. Fursat is not merely an event; it's an invitation to pause, reflect, and lose yourself in the timeless beauty of Ghazals.

At the heart of this gathering is Manish Chhabra, a self-taught aficionado of Ghazals, whose love for poetry isn't inherited but passionately cultivated over the years. His journey into Hindustani poetry has been one of discovery, devotion, and deep introspection.

With every verse, he unravels the intricate meanings, the unspoken emotions, and the lingering echoes of some of the greatest poets of our time — including Ahmed Faraz, Parveen Shakir, Nida Fazli, and more — offering a glimpse into their worlds where love, longing, and life intertwine effortlessly with poetic grace.
