Our Menu

Food & Beverages

From gourmet sandwiches, burgers, pizzas, and pasta to refreshing coolers, all-day breakfast, and rich Indian, Chinese, and Continental mains — every bite crafted to perfection with fresh ingredients.

☕ Beverages

Coffee Crafted to Perfection

At KlayBar, every cup of coffee is a masterpiece, brewed fresh to perfection. Whether you crave the rich warmth of a hot espresso or the refreshing chill of a cold brew, our artisanal blends promise a delightful experience. Savor the aroma, embrace the flavor, and let every sip awaken your senses!

🍵 Teas

Brewing Freshness, One Cup at a Time

Our freshly brewed teas at KlayBar bring the perfect balance of aroma and flavor, crafted to refresh and rejuvenate. From soothing hot herbal infusions to invigorating iced tea blends, each cup is brewed with the finest ingredients to deliver a pure and authentic tea experience. Sip, savor, and let the flavors unfold!

🍹 Coolers & Mocktails

Sip, Chill & Indulge

Experience a perfect blend of taste and refreshment with our coolers, crafted to suit every mood. From vibrant mocktails to indulgent milkshakes and nourishing healthy drinks, we offer both classy and classic options. Whether you're craving something rich and creamy or light and revitalizing, our signature coolers are designed to keep you refreshed and delighted!

🍳 All-Day Breakfast

Rise, Dine & Shine

Kickstart your day with KlayBar's lip-smacking all-day breakfast, crafted to satisfy every craving. From hearty classics to wholesome delights, our menu offers a perfect balance of flavor and freshness. Whether you're in the mood for a light bite or an indulgent feast, our all-day breakfast is designed to keep you energized and satisfied!

🍕 Mains

Bite into Bliss

Indulge in a delightful selection of handcrafted sandwiches, juicy burgers, cheesy pizzas, and comforting pastas at KlayBar. Every bite is packed with fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and the perfect balance of textures. Whether you're craving a quick bite or a hearty meal, our menu has something to satisfy every palate!

🥗 Starters & Salads

Fresh, Flavorful & Irresistible

Begin your meal with our tempting selection of starters, soul-warming soups, and refreshing salads. From crispy, flavorful bites to nourishing broths and garden-fresh greens, every dish is crafted to awaken your taste buds. Whether you're in the mood for something light or indulgent, our starters, soups, and salads set the perfect tone for a delightful dining experience!

🌿 We use fresh hydroponic greens in our dishes!
