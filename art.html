Art & DIY

Create, Express, Celebrate Art

Art is at the heart of KlayBar! From pottery and ceramic painting to creative DIY projects, we celebrate artistic expression in every form. Our space is designed to inspire and encourage creativity.

Experiences

What You Can Create Here

🏺 Pottery Wheel Sessions

Get hands-on at the pottery wheel and shape your own creations from raw clay. No experience needed — our guides will help you every step of the way.

🎨 Ceramic Painting

Choose from a variety of pre-made ceramic pieces and paint them in your own style. A perfect activity for individuals, couples, families, and groups.

✨ Creative DIY Projects

Beyond pottery, we offer a range of creative DIY art projects. Come explore, create, and immerse yourself in the joy of art!
