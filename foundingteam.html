Our Story

Meet the Founding Team

Klayworkz Barista – KlayBar Art Cafe is where creativity meets café culture! We blend art, coffee, & delicious food to create an inspiring space for all. More than just a café, we're a vibrant community where people connect, unwind, and express themselves.

Our Mission

Building a Community

Together, we're not just building a café — we're shaping a community where art, entrepreneurship, and sustainability come together. Join us on this journey!

Whether you're shaping clay, painting ceramics, or sipping a handcrafted brew, every visit is a chance to explore your creativity. Come for the experience, stay for the art, & leave with memories.
